## Confidence Levels

Confidence in conclusions is expressed at three levels:

**high** (0.7-1.0):
- Strong evidence directly supports the conclusion
- Multiple independent sources corroborate
- Mechanism is well-understood and verified
- No significant unresolved challenges

**medium** (0.4-0.7):
- Moderate evidence supports the conclusion
- Some gaps remain but can be filled
- Mechanism is plausible based on domain knowledge
- Minor challenges may be unresolved

**low** (0.0-0.4):
- Limited evidence available
- Significant gaps or uncertainties
- Mechanism requires assumptions
- Major challenges remain unresolved
