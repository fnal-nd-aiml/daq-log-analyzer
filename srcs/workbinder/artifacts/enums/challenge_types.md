## Challenge Types

Types of challenges raised during adversarial review:

- **logical_flaw**: Problems with the causal reasoning chain
- **weak_evidence**: Evidence that doesn't actually support the claims made
- **alternative**: Alternative explanations that better fit the evidence
- **missing**: Missing evidence that would strengthen or weaken the case

## Challenge Severity Levels

**HIGH**:
- Conclusion likely wrong
- Evidence contradicts claims
- Causal chain is impossible
- Alternative explanation is more likely

**MEDIUM**:
- Conclusion needs strengthening
- Evidence is circumstantial
- Gaps can be filled with additional investigation

**LOW**:
- Minor improvement possible
- Interpretation may vary
- Non-critical assumptions present
